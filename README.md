## ✨ What is TerraVerse?

TerraVerse is a free, beautifully animated encyclopedia of the natural world, the cosmos and human history. Each realm is its own living world. Fireflies drift through the jungle, light rays shimmer through the ocean, birds cross a sunset sky, stars twinkle in deep space and ancient glyphs float through the Languages realm.

Every entry goes deep, covering history, evolution, species, behaviour, DNA and the full story of what we know. Every page also connects to the world's open knowledge, so the encyclopedia never runs out.

## 🔬 Inside Every Entry

- **Quick facts** and classification at a glance
- **Animated timelines** of history and evolution
- **Types, species and relatives**, including extinct cousins
- **Life and behaviour**: diet, habitat, reproduction, communication
- **DNA and genome**: chromosome counts, genome size and genetic discoveries, with an animated double helix and karyotype
- **Conservation status** on the IUCN Red List scale
- **Extinction stories**: when, how and why a species disappeared
- **Alphabets** with flip cards for every letter and sign
- **Photo galleries** and the **complete article**, loaded live

## 🎨 Highlights

- 🌿 Fourteen hand-crafted animated themes with parallax landscapes
- 📈 Interactive visuals: Earth's 4.5-billion-year timeline, mass extinctions, mountain heights, the cosmic distance ladder, and the family tree of the alphabet
- 🔍 Instant search across TerraVerse and millions of articles worldwide
- 📱 Fully responsive, designed for phones as much as big screens
- ♿ Respects reduced-motion preferences
- 🔒 Encyclopedia data is stored in an encrypted database

## 🛠️ Built With

Pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks: a custom particle engine, procedural SVG scenery, the Web Crypto API and a lightweight SQLite reader.
